    How to run Round 2

    1. Before: add every registered team in Teams & codes, pick each team’s brand × product (or press Auto-assign), set the team code, and tell teams their exact team name and the code.
    2. Rehearse once with Practice mode ON, then press Reset round and switch Practice mode OFF.
    3. On the day: teams open the team link, type their team name and code, and land on the waiting screen.
    4. In Live round → Game schedule, press ▶ Start all teams. Every team starts at the same moment. Each team’s brand time capsule then opens in three steps: Lock 1 (unscramble the city) opens The Faces, Lock 2 (a question answered inside The Faces) opens The Noise, and the vault opens The Shelves. Nothing opens by itself: if a team is stuck, press ⏭ Next stage on their row. Every team row has its own controls: start now, pause/resume, next stage, go to any stage, stop, restart and reset.
    5. Logged-out teams appear in a red alert. Press Let back in; their screen unlocks by itself with progress kept.
    6. Use the timer and message buttons as you go. Press End round when time is up.

    Vault code for every team: 87634. City and question answers are in the case-study table above.
