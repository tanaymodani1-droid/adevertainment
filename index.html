    How to run Round 2

    1. Before: add every registered team in Teams & codes, pick each team’s brand × product (or press Auto-assign), set the team code, and tell teams their exact team name and the code.
    2. Rehearse once with Practice mode ON, then press Reset round and switch Practice mode OFF.
    3. On the day: teams open the team link, type their team name and code, and land on the waiting screen.
    4. In Live round → Game schedule, put teams in order with ↑ ↓ and press ▶ Start sequence. Team 1 starts now, the next team after the gap (7 minutes by default), and so on. Each team’s capsule then opens by itself: lid + Compartment 1 (The Faces), Compartment 2 (The Noise) after 7 minutes, Compartment 3 (The Shelves) after 14, and the design studio after 20, for a 20-minute deck. Every team row has its own controls: start now, pause/resume, next stage, +1 minute, go to any stage, stop, restart and reset.
    5. Logged-out teams appear in a red alert. Press Let back in; their screen unlocks by itself with progress kept.
    6. Use the timer and message buttons as you go. Press End round when time is up.

    Round 2 is 40 minutes: the capsule opens over 20 minutes on the game laptop, then the studio opens on the design laptop for the last 20 minutes. Decks must show People, Promotion and Place.
